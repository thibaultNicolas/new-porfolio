Verdict System Pages Moves

nicolas-thibault.ca  ×  dennissnellenberg.com

His site is quiet.
Yours is busy.

Dennis designed a system and then got out of the way. You designed a collection of effects and then asked them to do the talking. The gap is not talent or stack. It is editing, restraint, and a single idea of what the page is for.

00 — The point

He sells taste. You sell effort.

A freelance designer’s homepage has one job: make a stranger believe this person has better judgment than the last ten they interviewed. Dennis does that in the first viewport. You do it somewhere around the third glass card, if at all.

Dennis

  • One custom sans, one weight (450). Size does the hierarchy.
  • Five colors. Blue is a signature, not a theme.
  • Hero is a composition: name, role, place, face, CTA.
  • Work is a typographic list. Images appear on hover.
  • The footer is a destination, not a sitemap.
  • Motion is magnetic, slow, and rare.

Feels like a person who would say no to a bad idea.

You

  • Two fonts. Weight 800 on almost every heading.
  • Gradients, orbs, grain, glass, peach, and dusk-blue — all at once.
  • Hero is a title card. No face, no CTA, no place.
  • Work is a stack of rounded screenshot cards with overlays.
  • Footer has empty columns and a ghost wordmark.
  • Motion is everywhere, so none of it means anything.

Feels like a talented developer decorating a résumé.

His palette is a decision. Yours is a moodboard.

02 — Type

Stop using weight as hierarchy.

Dennis Sans at weight 450, always. Homepage display is 216px / 1.0 line-height. Styleguide H1 is ~88px. Labels are 10px uppercase. Body is ~17–21px. Tracking is normal. Nothing is extrabold. Nothing is tight.

Dennis

Display Snellenberg— 216px · 450
H1 Heading H1 88px · 450
H4 A sentence, not a slogan. 33px · 450
H5 Recent work 10px · uppercase

You

H1 full-stack 128px · 800 · −0.05em
H2 À propos 96px · 800 · −0.06em
H3 Lexya ~60px · 800
UI Voir le projet 10px · bold · tracked

Plus Jakarta ExtraBold with −0.05em tracking is a 2022 Webflow / Awwwards clone tell. It shouts. Dennis whispers at 216px and still wins, because the letterforms are allowed to be themselves. Pick one grotesque. Use two weights, maximum. Let size and color carry rank.

What he includes

  • Visible text nav. No hamburger on desktop.
  • Role as a human sentence, not a job title lockup.
  • Name as the product. The em dash is a pause, not a flourish.
  • A photograph. A person, not a gradient.

What you omit

  • Low-contrast dusk-blue on pale cyan. The type is large and still timid.

04 — Information architecture

Cut the homepage in half.

Dennis has one idea per scroll. You have a full LinkedIn profile, a case-study grid, a tech inventory, and a process diagram before anyone is asked to write you. That is a pitch deck, not a homepage.

Dennis · ~4,800px

  1. Hero — identity + CTA 1 screen
  2. Intro — manifesto + About ½ screen
  3. Work list — 4 names 1 screen
  4. More work — one button small
  5. Footer — let’s work together 1 screen

You · ~11,600px

  1. Hero — title only 1 screen
  2. About — 3 paragraphs + stats long
  3. Experience — 6 stacked cards cut
  4. Projects — 3 glass cards keep, rewrite
  5. Tech stack — laundry list cut
  6. Process — 4 generic steps cut
  7. Footer — sitemap + ghost type rewrite

05 — Work

Cards are a confession of distrust.

Dennis trusts the name of the project. TWICE. The Damai. FABRIC™. Role and year sit in gray, on a hairline. Hover, and a magnetic “View” disc follows the cursor with the image inside it. The work is revealed, not wallpapered.

List, then media

  • Typography is the interface. ~76px names, weight 450.
  • One row: name — role — year.
  • Images are a reward for curiosity, not a default.
  • “More work 11” is a count, not a banner.
  • No tech pills. The craft is in the site you’re on.

Card, then more card

  • 16/10 screenshot in a 40px-radius glass frame.
  • Hover overlay: “Voir le projet” in tracked caps. 2018 Dribbble.
  • A second glass panel for the text. Why is copy in a card?
  • Tech chips (Next.js, TypeScript) as if the hiring manager needs badges.
  • Alternating left/right layout. Template rhythm. Predictable.

06 — The rest of the scroll

About, experience, stack, process, footer.

Intro

  • One manifesto line at 33px: “Helping brands to stand out in the digital era.”
  • One supporting paragraph. Then a round “About me”.
  • He does not explain why he is different. The site already did.

Footer

  • “Let’s work together” as display type.
  • Giant magnetic Get in touch.
  • Email and phone as large pill links.
  • Version. Local time. Socials. The site feels alive and current.

About + stats

  • Three paragraphs of self-positioning. “Je demande pourquoi avant comment.”
  • “100% satisfaction” is a claim nobody believes. Cut it.
  • Glass stat cards under a grain overlay. Decoration on decoration.

Experience / stack / process

  • Pinned rainbow cards for every internship. Homepage is not a CV.
  • Stack lists jQuery next to Next.js. That is not curation.
  • Process: Découverte → Conception → Développement → Livraison. Agency filler.
  • Footer: unused grid cell, italic line, peach underline, 20vw ghost “THIBAULT”.

07 — Motion and craft

He animates objects. You animate the page.

Dennis: locomotive smooth scroll, a multilingual preloader, magnetic buttons with a 0.7s cubic-bezier(.7, 0, .3, 1), hover image that tracks the mouse. Four tricks, done perfectly. You: GSAP + Framer Motion, orb drift, text reveal, card pin-stack, image scrub, hover scale, drawer slide, marquee. The page never sits still, so nothing can land.

Also: his easing is a brand token (--animation-primary). Yours is a different curve in every file. Pick one bezier and make it law.

08 — What to do

Steal the system. Do not clone the site.

Ranked. Do these in order. Each one makes the next one cheaper.

01

Kill the atmosphere layer

Remove radial gradients, floating orbs, grain SVGs, glass-panel, backdrop-blur, and box shadows from the homepage. Flat surfaces. Alternate charcoal and white the way he does. Your type will suddenly look twice as expensive.

02

One typeface, two weights

Drop the ExtraBold / −0.05em lockup. Keep Plus Jakarta or switch to a single grotesque. Weights: 450–500 for everything, 600 only if you must. Hierarchy = size + color. Labels = 10–11px, slight tracking, uppercase, gray.

03

Rebuild the hero as a layout

Visible nav (Work, About, Contact). A one-line role. Your name or a sharper claim as display type. A real photograph. Location: Québec. One round CTA: “Écrivez-moi” / “Get in touch”. That is the whole first screen.

04

Homepage = Hero, Intro, Work, Footer

Move experience, stack, and process off the home. Experience belongs on About. Stack belongs in project pages, if anywhere. Process is optional and currently generic — rewrite it or delete it.

05

Work as a typographic list

Name, role, year. Hairline rules. On hover, a magnetic disc with the project image (you already have GSAP). No glass cards, no tech pills, no “Voir le projet →” overlay. Let Lexya be a word, not a widget.

06

One accent, used like a signature

Keep burnt peach or pick a blue. Not both. Use it on the magnetic button, one character in the kicker, and link hover. If it appears more than three times per viewport, it has stopped meaning anything.

07

Make the footer the close

“Travaillons ensemble.” Display size. One giant round CTA. Email as a large pill. Local time (America/Toronto). LinkedIn / GitHub as text, not icons. Delete the unused grid cell and the ghost THIBAULT.

08

Write less, and write harder

Replace the three-paragraph about with one sentence you would say out loud. Cut “100% satisfaction” and “20+ projets”. Cut “Centré Utilisateur • Performance • …” marquee. Specificity is the aesthetic. “Lead on a Québec textbook platform” beats “I think like a product builder”.

Do not

  • Copy his custom font, magnetic easing, or “Code by …” credit. That is his brand, not a kit.
  • Go dark just because he is dark. Dark works because everything else is quiet. Dark + orbs + glass is worse.
  • Add a preloader that says Bonjour / Hello until the rest of the site is as considered as the loader.
  • Keep every section and “simplify” the CSS. The sections are the problem.

09 — If you only change five files

A practical cut list.

Touch

  • globals.css — strip utility noise, set 5 tokens, one easing.
  • Hero.tsx — layout, photo, CTA, kill orbs.
  • Header.tsx — desktop text links; drawer on mobile only.
  • Projects.tsx — list + magnetic hover, not cards.
  • Footer.tsx — display CTA, pills, local time.

Remove from home

  • Experience.tsx → About page.
  • TechStack.tsx → delete or tuck into About.
  • Process.tsx → rewrite or drop.
  • About stats row → one sentence, or nothing.
  • Framer Motion in the header if GSAP already owns motion.
